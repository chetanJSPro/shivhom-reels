## 6. Audio strategy (this is the growth hack)

The song itself can become a trending audio page — that's how songs blow up on Reels:

- **Option A (simple):** Upload as-is. Each reel carries the song audio; IG may auto-group them.
- **Option B (pro, recommended):** Post one **"FULL SONG" reel first** (any visual, full 5:38 audio, caption: "पूरा गाना सुनो 🔱"). IG creates an *original audio page* for it. Then upload each of the 19 reels with **"Use audio" → select that SHIVHOM audio** and slide the selector to the same segment (timestamps are in `reels.json`). Now all 19 reels point to ONE audio page — every view compounds on a single trending page, and anyone can remix it.

## 7. Growth tips that actually work for devotional reels

- **First 3 seconds decide everything** — that's why hook text pops at 3.6s with the catchiest line of that segment.
- **Loop the best reels:** Reel 17 (Har Har Mahadev) and Reel 19 (ॐ नमः शिवाय) are comment-bait — people type the mantra in comments. Reply + like every comment.
- **Reply with questions** ("किसको dedicate करोगे ये गाना?") — comments with replies get re-surfaced.
- **Cross-post:** same video to YouTube Shorts, FB Reels, and WhatsApp status → YouTube pushes Shorts to the 5:38 full video automatically (that's your view pipeline).
- **Pin the song's best line as a comment** ("तेरे भीतर का महादेव 🔱") so people repeat it.
- **Consistency > perfection:** 1 reel daily at the same time for 3 weeks builds an audience for the full song.
- Don't use more than 6–8 hashtags on IG (they now read keywords from the caption text itself; your captions already contain the right Hindi keywords).
